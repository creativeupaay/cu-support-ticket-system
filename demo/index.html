Client Application Demonstration

Live Client App with Embedded Support Widget

This demo shows how flexible SupportHub is. You can trigger support from a floating corner launcher, a custom styled button, a normal text link, or embed the intake form directly inline!

1. Floating Widget (Corner)

Notice the floating launcher at the bottom-right labeled "Get Support". Its color, position, and text are customizable.

2. Custom Button & Text Link

Any custom button or link matching data-support-trigger or href="#support" opens the widget instantly.

3. Direct Inline Component

You can also embed the intake desk directly inside your page layout without any popover modal, as shown in the section below.

Direct Embed Component

Embedded Intake Form (Inline Mode)
